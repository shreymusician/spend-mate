PROMPT FOR THE LOGO DESIGN AGENT
=================================

ROLE
You are a senior brand and icon designer. Design a new logo for a mobile-first money-tracking app called "SpendMate". Deliver production-ready SVG files and a short rationale. Do not use stock icons or templates; draw original shapes.


ABOUT THE PRODUCT
- Name: SpendMate. Tagline: "Know where your money goes."
- What it does: a person writes down what they spend and why, sees clear monthly summaries, and can choose to share selected entries with a parent or family member (who can only view them).
- Audience: students and young adults tracking pocket money, and their parents. Many parents are not tech-savvy, so the logo must feel friendly, calm and trustworthy, never flashy or "crypto".
- Market: India (amounts are in rupees), but the logo must not rely on a currency symbol.
- It is a web app used on phones and installed to the home screen like a native iPhone app. The design language is Apple iOS: clean, soft rounded shapes, frosted glass, subtle depth, restrained gradients.


WHAT IS WRONG WITH THE CURRENT LOGO (replace it entirely)
- It is a green gradient rounded square with a white rupee sign (₹) inside. That is generic: it looks like every finance app and any template.
- It is not memorable, says nothing about "mate" (companionship / sharing) or "tracking", and is tied to one currency.


DESIGN GOAL
Create ONE distinctive symbol (a "mark") plus a wordmark, that feels modern, warm and unmistakably SpendMate. It should be recognisable at 16 px (browser tab) and look premium at 1024 px (app icon).

Concept directions to explore (pick the strongest and justify it; you may combine two):
1. "Two paths, one line": two simple shapes (the person and their mate/parent) joined by a single flowing line that also reads as a spending trend or an "S". Suggests tracking plus sharing.
2. "Ledger leaf": an abstract bookmark / receipt fold that doubles as a leaf or upward stroke, hinting at money growing and being noted.
3. "Overlapping rings": two overlapping rounded forms (like a friendly handshake or a pair of coins seen from the side) whose overlap is a different shade, hinting at "shared view".
4. A monogram "S" built from two mirrored, rounded strokes, with a small notch or dot that signals "an entry noted".
Whatever you choose: one idea, few shapes, bold and simple. A person should be able to redraw it from memory.


MUST AVOID (cliches and traps)
- Rupee, dollar, euro or any currency sign; piggy banks; coin stacks; wallets; banknotes; credit cards; padlocks; shields; rockets; bar-chart icons; magnifying glasses.
- A plain letter "S" in a circle or square, or a lightly modified existing font glyph.
- Purple-to-blue "startup gradient", neon, glassy 3D bevels, glows, drop shadows baked into the artwork, or more than 3 colours in the mark.
- Anything that resembles the logos of Apple, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Splitwise, Mint, YNAB, Revolut, Cash App or other existing apps. Check for lookalikes before delivering.
- Text inside the symbol. Thin strokes under 4% of the mark height (they vanish when small).


COLOUR (must match the app)
- Brand gradient used across the app: #34E3B0 (mint) to #0FA37F (emerald) to #0A7A8D (deep teal), at about 140 degrees.
- Light theme: background #F2F2F7 with white cards and near-black text #0E0F12.
- Dark theme: background #000000 with #1C1C1E cards and near-white text #F5F5F7.
- The mark should use the gradient (or a flat #0FA37F fallback). You may add ONE small accent in warm amber #FFC46B (used sparingly, for example a single dot) if it helps the idea.
- The logo must pass contrast on both light and dark backgrounds. Provide a one-colour version that works in pure white and pure black.


TYPOGRAPHY (wordmark)
- Wordmark text: "SpendMate" (capital S, capital M, no space).
- Style: a clean geometric or humanist sans, medium to semibold weight, slightly tight letter-spacing, in the spirit of SF Pro / Inter / Manrope / Plus Jakarta Sans. Rounded terminals are welcome. Convert text to outlines in the final SVG so it renders identically everywhere.
- Consider making the "M" or the join between "Spend" and "Mate" echo the symbol.


DELIVERABLES (create these files)
Write everything into the project folder: client/public/brand/ (create it).
1. mark.svg                 - symbol only, full colour (gradient), transparent background, square viewBox 0 0 512 512, 8% safe padding.
2. mark-mono-black.svg and mark-mono-white.svg - one-colour versions.
3. logo-horizontal.svg      - symbol + wordmark side by side, for light backgrounds.
4. logo-horizontal-dark.svg - the same, tuned for dark backgrounds.
5. app-icon.svg             - 1024x1024 full-bleed square with the mark centred (iOS rounds the corners itself, so do NOT draw rounded corners or a border). Keep the important artwork inside the central 80%.
6. app-icon-maskable.svg    - same, but all important artwork inside the central 60% circle (for Android maskable icons).
7. favicon.svg              - simplified mark that stays legible at 16x16 and 32x32 (fewer details than mark.svg).
8. PNG exports of the app icon: icon-512.png, icon-192.png, apple-touch-icon-180.png, favicon-32.png.
9. A small preview page client/public/brand/preview.html that shows every file on light and dark backgrounds, and at 16, 32, 64 and 180 px.
10. brand-notes.md: the concept in 3 to 5 sentences, the colour values, clear-space and minimum-size rules (for example "minimum 16 px"), and 3 "do not" examples.

SVG QUALITY RULES
- Hand-written, clean SVG: no embedded raster images, no external fonts, no filters or blur (they render inconsistently), no clip-paths unless unavoidable. Use <linearGradient> for the brand gradient. Use simple paths and rounded joins. Keep each file under 6 KB.
- Use viewBox, no fixed width/height attributes. Add <title> and role="img" for accessibility.
- Everything must render the same in Chrome, Safari on iPhone, and Firefox.


HOW TO CHECK YOUR WORK
- Render the preview page and look at it at 16 px and 32 px: the idea must still be readable.
- Squint test: blur your eyes; you should still see one clear shape.
- Show the mark on the real app screens (the landing page and the blue-green "Money you have" card) mentally or via a mock-up image and confirm it does not clash.
- Compare against the "must avoid" list above.


INTEGRATION (after the design is approved by the user)
Only after delivering the files above and getting approval, update the app:
- client/index.html: favicon links, apple-touch-icon and theme-color.
- client/src/components/ui/Logo.tsx: replace the current gradient square with the new mark (keep the same size prop; inline the SVG so it themes properly).
- Landing page (client/src/pages/LandingPage.tsx) and the sign-in header already use <Logo />; confirm they still look right in light and dark mode.
- Do not change anything else in the app.


OUTPUT FORMAT
1. A short rationale (maximum 150 words): the concept, why it suits SpendMate, and how it avoids the cliches.
2. Two alternative concepts as quick sketches (mark only) so the user can choose, then fully develop the one the user picks.
3. The files listed in DELIVERABLES.
Ask the user at most one question, and only if something is truly blocking.
